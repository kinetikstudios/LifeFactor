## 🎯 About LifeFactor

**LifeFactor** is a modern, privacy-focused, and high-performance fitness application designed to help users log workouts and track nutrition effortlessly. Built with an **offline-first** architecture, the app ensures blazing-fast interactions and works seamlessly without relying on a central database server. Everything is stored locally, keeping your data entirely yours.

---

## ✨ Features

### 🏋️ Performance Dashboard
* **Workout Logging**: Quick and intuitive interface to log your daily training routines.
* **Progressive Overload Tracking**: Analyze your volume, sets, and reps over time with ultra-smooth analytics.
* **Daily Activity**: Monitor daily steps and weekly targets with dynamic goal rings.

### 🍎 Smart Nutrition Tracker
* **Macro Breakdown**: Track Calories, Proteins, Carbs, and Fats with an automated daily progress bar.
* **Tunisian Barcode Scanner**: Local camera integration leveraging *Google ML Kit* and *Open Food Facts* to instantly identify local products (optimized for `619` Tunisian barcodes).
* **AI Food Estimator**: Snap a picture of your plate to instantly estimate meal weight, calories, and macros using advanced vision models. Includes native support for Tunisian dishes (Couscous, Ojja, Llablabi).
* **Crowdsourced Backup**: Locally add unknown products to your personal database if they aren't found online.

### ⚡ Performance Optimized
* **60+ FPS Transitions**: Fully optimized views eliminating unnecessary re-renders during tab switching or exercise carousels.
* **Persistent States**: Remembers user choices natively, including a one-time safety disclaimer for the AI nutrition features.
